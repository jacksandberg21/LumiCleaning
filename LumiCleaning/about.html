Owner & operator

Hi, I'm Jack.

I'm 24 years old, originally from Utah, and currently a Computer Science major at BYU–Hawaiʻi. I started Lumi Cleaning LLC because I genuinely enjoy cleaning — there's something satisfying about walking into a messy space and leaving it spotless, and I bring that same care to every Airbnb turnover, home, and office I work on.

Being a student keeps me organized and detail-oriented, and running this business on the side means every job gets my full attention — no franchise script, no rushed crew, just consistent, careful work from someone who actually shows up when he says he will.

When I'm not cleaning or in class, you'll usually find me around Lāʻie and the North Shore. If you'd like to know more before booking, feel free to reach out directly.
